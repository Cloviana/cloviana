## What is Cloviana?

Cloviana is a B2B SaaS GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) platform. It helps B2B SaaS companies appear in AI-generated answers on platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini when buyers search for software recommendations.

The core problem: B2B buyers now research vendors by asking AI tools before they ever visit a website. If your product is not cited in those answers, you lose the deal before you even knew there was one.

Cloviana fixes that by continuously optimizing a company's AI search presence so their product shows up when it matters.

## Why Now?

AI search is changing how B2B buying decisions are made. Buyers ask ChatGPT "what's the best [software category] for [use case]" and act on the answer. Most SaaS companies have no presence in those answers, not because their product is bad, but because they have never optimized for it.

Traditional SEO agencies do not understand how LLMs synthesize and ground information. They rebrand SEO as "GEO" without knowing what they are actually doing. There is a real gap between what the market needs and what exists.

GEO requires understanding JSON-LD schema, LLM indexing behavior, content structure for AI retrieval, and how different AI platforms weight sources differently. That is a technical problem wrapped in a marketing outcome, which is exactly where Cloviana sits.

## The Product

Currently operating as service-as-software: Samuel (founder) acts as the human in the loop while the underlying system is being built toward a fully autonomous GEO agent.

The roadmap:
1. Free AI Visibility Audit Tool (in progress): lead gen tool where a company enters their name and category, then gets a visibility score showing where they appear, or do not appear, across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini versus competitors.
2. Client Dashboard: built after the first 2 to 3 clients are onboarded.
3. Fully Autonomous Agent: the long-term product vision.

## Who It's For

ICP: B2B SaaS founders and growth or marketing leaders at companies doing $500k to $5M ARR, 1 to 50 employees, relying on organic inbound, with no internal engineering resources to solve this themselves.

Core pitch: "When your customers ask ChatGPT for the best software in your niche, it recommends your competitor. Cloviana fixes that."

Founders are the primary target until Cloviana has enough proof points to speak to marketing leaders.

## Positioning

Cloviana is an autonomous agent, not an agency and not a tool. No dashboards to manage, no developers needed, no agency retainer relationship.

"GEO" as a term is used lower on the page because the ICP does not recognize it yet. Hero copy should speak in plain, buyer-centric language the ICP already understands.
