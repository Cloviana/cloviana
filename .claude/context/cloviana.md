# Cloviana — Product Brief

## What it is (one paragraph)
Cloviana is an AI-automated SEO platform for Shopify brands that acts like a productized SEO agency in software form. It connects to a store’s catalog, finds commercial keywords, builds a content strategy, and generates product-led blog posts (problem–solution and comparison formats) designed to rank in Google and AI search engines. It supports scheduled “autopilot” publishing so merchants can produce consistent search-optimized content without hiring an agency or managing writers, with the goal of compounding organic traffic and revenue from high-intent queries.

## Thesis and positioning
Cloviana replaces the repeatable SEO agency deliverable with software:
- Keyword research → content plan → product-led content production → consistent publishing

Analogy:
- marketer.com aims to replace agencies for paid growth automation.
- Cloviana targets the equivalent “agency replacement” for SEO content automation (strategy + writing + publishing), specifically for Shopify.

## What Cloviana does (scope)
Cloviana automates:
- Commercial keyword discovery (purchase-intent focused)
- Topic selection and prioritization (structured plan)
- Product-led blog generation (not generic content)
- Publishing workflow + scheduling (“autopilot”)

Cloviana is not (core promise):
- Full technical SEO consulting/projects
- PR / digital PR
- Manual link-building programs
- Highly bespoke, ongoing human strategy work

## Who it’s for (ICP)
Best-fit customers:
- Shopify DTC brands with consistent sales
- Founder/marketing generalist teams with no in-house SEO operator
- Want organic search to become a real channel over 6–12 months
- Want a “set-and-forget” content engine tied to products and revenue

Catalog fit (important nuance):
- Ideal: 10–300 SKUs (more angles, more internal linking, more keyword clusters)
- Still possible: 3–9 SKUs if they have clear demand + strong use-cases + willingness to invest in SEO
- Weak fit: 1–2 SKUs or stores that won’t expand the catalog (content angles get exhausted fast)

## Who it’s NOT for
Usually poor fit:
- Brand new / low-sales stores expecting instant results
- Teams that already have an SEO lead + writers producing consistently
- Brands primarily needing deep technical SEO, PR, or link-building as the main lever
- Teams that want heavy manual control vs autopilot

## Simple qualification questions
- Are you already getting consistent sales?
- Do you have (or plan to have) a real catalog to support content angles?
- Do you want SEO to compound over the next 6–12 months?
- Can you publish blogs on Shopify (permission + willingness)?

## Core capabilities (current)
- Shopify integration + product catalog sync
- Commercial keyword discovery + topic ideas
- Content strategy generation (what to write, prioritized)
- Blog generation using product-led templates:
  - Problem–solution
  - Comparison
- Autopilot scheduling/publishing

## High-level workflow
1) Connect Shopify store
2) Sync products (optionally collections)
3) Generate keyword + topic strategy
4) Create outlines + draft blogs (product-led)
5) Publish now or schedule via autopilot

## Value proposition (why it wins)
- Agency-like output without agency cost/overhead
- Consistent publishing solves the “we stopped doing SEO” failure mode
- Content stays tied to products/catalog (commercial intent)
- Designed for compounding organic visibility and revenue

## Pricing and trial (current model)
- Starter: $39/month
  - 4 blogs/month
  - Autopilot included
- Trial: 14 days
  - Up to 2 published blogs during trial
  - Paywall after trial ends or after 2 publishes (whichever first)
  - Draft generation can remain free; publishing is the conversion gate

## Summary line
Cloviana is a Shopify SEO content engine that automates the repeatable work an SEO agency charges for—commercial keyword research, product-led blog creation, and consistent publishing—so merchants can build compounding organic traffic without paying £2k–£6k/month for an agency.
