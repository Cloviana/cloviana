# Cloviana — Product Context

## What is Cloviana?

Cloviana is a B2B SaaS GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) platform. It helps B2B SaaS companies appear in AI-generated answers on platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini when buyers search for software recommendations.

The core problem: B2B buyers now research vendors by asking AI tools before they ever visit a website. If your product isn't cited in those answers, you lose the deal before you even knew there was one.

Cloviana fixes that — continuously optimizing a company's AI search presence so their product shows up when it matters.

---

## Why Now?

AI search is changing how B2B buying decisions are made. Buyers ask ChatGPT "what's the best [software category] for [use case]" and act on the answer. Most SaaS companies have no presence in those answers — not because their product is bad, but because they've never optimized for it.

Traditional SEO agencies don't understand how LLMs synthesize and ground information. They rebrand SEO as "GEO" without knowing what they're actually doing. There's a real gap between what the market needs and what exists.

GEO requires understanding JSON-LD schema, LLM indexing behaviour, content structure for AI retrieval, and how different AI platforms weight sources differently. That's a technical problem wrapped in a marketing outcome — which is exactly where Cloviana sits.

---

## The Product

Currently operating as service-as-software — Samuel (founder) acts as the human-in-the-loop while the underlying system is being built toward a fully autonomous GEO agent.

The roadmap:
1. **Free AI Visibility Audit Tool** (in progress) — lead gen tool where a company enters their name and category, and gets a visibility score showing where they appear (or don't) across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini vs. competitors.
2. **Client Dashboard** — built after the first 2–3 clients are onboarded.
3. **Fully Autonomous Agent** — the long-term product vision.

---

## Who It's For

**ICP:** B2B SaaS founders and growth/marketing leaders at companies doing $500k–$5M ARR, 1–50 employees, relying on organic inbound, with no internal engineering resources to solve this themselves.

The pitch is simple: *"When your customers ask ChatGPT for the best software in your niche, it recommends your competitor. Cloviana fixes that."*

Founders are the primary target until Cloviana has proof points to speak to marketing leaders.

---

## Positioning

Cloviana is an **autonomous agent**, not an agency and not a tool. No dashboards to manage, no developers needed, no agency retainer relationship.

"GEO" as a term is used lower on the page — the ICP doesn't recognise it yet. Hero copy speaks in plain buyer-centric language the ICP already understands.
