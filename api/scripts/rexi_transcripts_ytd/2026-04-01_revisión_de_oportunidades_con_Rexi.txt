### Pain Points by Area

### Mariano García — Leasing, Hipotecas, Prendarios & Seguros

- Conciliación rubros contables de leasing
  - Leasing does not run on BanTotal; runs on a separate system called Accord that interacts with BanTotal
  - Data between the two systems frequently doesn’t reconcile — errors and mismatches
  - Second control: invoices loaded in system vs. invoices paid by accounts payable
  - Leasing also carries associated costs (infractions, patents, insurance, notary fees) — mismatches between what each system reports
  - Today: large amount of time spent reviewing items that are already correct, instead of focusing on exceptions
- Conciliación seguros (Supervielle Seguros — internal broker)
  - Process: match open accounting entry (expected claim payment) against actual payment received from insurer
  - Entirely manual, inconsistencies in the data
  - Same problem: too much time spent on items that are fine; wants to focus only on exceptions
- Cadence: no need for daily reconciliation
  - Leasing: weekly
  - Seguros: monthly

### Santiago Messina — Operaciones Activas

- Products in scope: préstamos, acuerdos, plafones, descuento de facturas y cheques
- Control contable (préstamos y factoring)
  - Semi-automated today via Excel with macros: loads daily movement list, flags differences
  - Rubro must always net to zero (account + operation level)
  - When differences appear, one person investigates each one manually and contacts the responsible party to regularize
- Préstamo con código de descuento
  - Product for municipal employees (Municipalidad de Mendoza) — no bank account required
  - Installments deducted from payroll by the employer entity, transferred to an internal bank account
  - Manual process: match each installment from the entity’s file against the internal account, then debit
  - Product currently discontinued but the bank wants to revive it
- Carga de préstamos y acuerdos a empresas
  - Requests arrive via a workflow system (migrating from Gestar to CRM)
  - All data entered manually into Accord/BanTotal: client account, amount, first installment, rate, number of installments
  - Exceptions (credit or rate) require manual routing to internal areas before disbursement
  - Post-disbursement: upload to Tux (document repository) — this step will be resolved by the CRM migration

### Vilma Furlan — Back Office Mesa de Dinero & Sociedad Depositaria FCI

- Products: títulos, call money, plazos fijos, cuentas remuneradas, suscripciones y rescates de fondos comunes de inversión (FCI)
- All with institutional clients, counterparties, or specific clients
- Cuentas remuneradas (clientes institucionales)
  - Managed via Excel and email today
  - Payments are automated, but process has room to reduce errors and rework
- Fondos Comunes de Inversión (FCI)
  - Reconciliation: BanTotal vs. Visual Fondos
  - Currently uses macros; daily reconciliation at open, close, and intraday
- BYMA Clearing
  - New system going live \~24 April — will require a new reconciliation/adaptation process
  - Deferred: Vilma flagged this as a post-implementation item
- Control contable: additional items flagged for a follow-up conversation

### Rodrigo Robles — Onboarding / Apertura de Cuentas Empresas

- No reconciliation or accounting control use cases — out of scope for this phase
- Two main pain points noted for context:
  - Bonificación de cuentas: manual daily entry into BanTotal core to apply account fee waivers (12–24 month campaigns)
  - Carga de facultades en Design: manual loading of legal powers, mandates, and authorized signatories from external law firm dictámenes
- AI for document processing (estatutos, contratos sociales → auto-generate dictámenes) already in progress internally for \~3 years
- Eduardo noted Rodrigo’s area is typically the entry point for other AI vendors — already has competing projects

### Leandro Araujo — Middle Office Cash

- Control contable: liquidaciones rechazadas generally net to zero within 24 hours; bots already handle this — not seen as an urgent pain point today
- Remaining pain points are operational/systemic rather than reconciliation:
  - Plan sueldos: file validation (record count vs. totals) to prevent duplicate processing
  - Débito directo: same manual file-loading risk
  - Operaciones cash: closing a collection account requires closing the current account separately — generates unnecessary admin charges
  - Comercios: no system notification when a merchant cancels their service, causing timing mismatches and potential bank losses
- Assessment: use cases are more about file manipulation and systemic controls than reconciliation — lower priority for this phase

### Julio Arandía — Medios de Pago (Tarjetas)

- Baja integral de tarjeta de crédito
  - No unified motor today to handle all inbound cancellation triggers (ANSES deceased, voluntary client cancellation, bank-initiated)
  - Motor needs to evaluate each case: serialize, bulletin, or block the card
  - Requested since 2022; never prioritized
- Reversa de pagos de TC
  - Rules should live in CRM; today the daily review is fully manual
- Desconocimiento de consumos TC y TD
  - 6 resources dedicated to this process — high capacity cost
  - TC: provisional credit issued, then resolved based on processor outcome (local vs. international timelines differ)
  - TD: 5-day resolution window; macros used to track partial closures until all 5 claims are finalized
- Orquestador de paquetes de productos
  - No system today takes a daily snapshot of a client’s full product set across cores (SmartOpen, BanTotal, etc.)
  - Package assignment is a one-time snapshot at onboarding — never updated dynamically
  - Consequences: clients may be over- or under-charged; bank may be waiving fees it shouldn’t, or charging for packages that no longer apply
  - Seen internally as a complex problem; Eduardo confirmed it’s implementable
- Note: Julio’s area is mid-migration (SmartOpen → Anywhere, which includes an accounting module) — contable reconciliation work paused pending migration; no short-term opportunity here
