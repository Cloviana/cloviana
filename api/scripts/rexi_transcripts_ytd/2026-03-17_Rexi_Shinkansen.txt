- Shinkansen specializes in bank connectivity for Latin American businesses

- Initial partnership interest from both sides

  - Shinkansen interested in Rexi’s reconciliation capabilities for their clients

### Shinkansen’s Business Model & Capabilities

- Two main pricing models:
  1. Transactional fees (fixed cost per transaction regardless of amount)
  2. SaaS model for corporates (based on number of banks and modules)
- Bank connection expertise:
  - Connect to any bank interface (host-to-host, APIs, web services)
  - Abstract integration complexity for clients
  - Even help banks develop their own API infrastructure
- Current reconciliation offering limited to large corporates only
  - Requires ERP integrations and custom projects
  - Not available for smaller clients who request it

- Panco expressed skepticism about startup partnerships
  - Prefers case-by-case collaboration over top-down alliances
  - Each company has urgent priorities that take precedence
- Rexi’s strategic position:
  - Wants to control bank connectors long-term as part of core product
  - Views Shinkansen as go-to-market acceleration, not permanent solution
- Complementary strengths identified:
  - Shinkansen: bank data extraction
  - Rexi: other data sources (ERPs, Stripe, etc.) and reconciliation logic

### Potential Collaboration Areas

- Bank integration acceleration:
  - Rexi could leverage Shinkansen’s existing connections when needed
  - Shinkansen could help with new bank integrations on demand
- Reconciliation gap for Shinkansen’s tech clients:
  - Startup clients request reconciliation but don’t get it currently
  - Potential reseller or white-label opportunity for Rexi
