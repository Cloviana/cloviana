### Rexi Product Overview & Market Position

- Automated reconciliation platform for financial services
  - Compares transactional data across core banking, ledger, payment processors
  - Identifies discrepancies, lost transactions, refunds, backend failures
- Current clients include US fintechs, payment processors, Buenbit
  - SuperVL nearly signed $400K contract (delayed due to core system change to STQ)
  - Buenbit active implementation in progress
- Main competitor: Symmetric (Colombian, 5-7 years in market)
  - Galicia, Naranja X, Mercado Pago clients
  - Mercado Pago pays $10M+ annually
  - Galicia Ventures invested in Symmetric
  - Voila terminated Symmetric contract last year to build internal solution

### Bain’s Market Perspective on AI in Banking

- Current AI adoption very diffuse across banks
  - Everyone doing small projects without clear strategy
  - Most valuable use case: legacy code modernization using AI
  - Customer service automation promising but cultural change required
- Vendor selection patterns:
  - Banks prefer established players (Salesforce Einstein, Google, Microsoft)
  - Niche vendors need strong logo references to gain traction
  - Risk aversion high - need proven success stories
- Embedded solutions for merchant reconciliation potentially easier sell
  - Lower risk profile than internal bank systems
  - Stone (Brazil) and other acquirers building/buying these capabilities

### Technical Implementation & Pricing Models

- Two deployment options:
  1. SaaS model for fintechs/payment processors
  2. On-premise/client-hosted for banks (higher security requirements)
- SOC 2 Type 2 certified, pursuing SOC 1 and ISO 27001
- Pricing example (SuperVL): $400K upfront, $6-12K annual retainer
  - Covers 26 money flows, deployment, first-year implementation
  - Additional flows charged separately
- Implementation timeline: <1 month for assessment and value demonstration
  - Speed limited by client data access, not Rexi capabilities
