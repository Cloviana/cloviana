- Focus on 2 reconciliation workflows: credit cards and airline/non-airline
  - Credit cards: highest volume, significant manual effort investment
  - Air/non-air: similar characteristics, good coverage of use cases
- 8-week pilot timeline with parallel processing to current workflows
- Success metrics identified:
  1. Manual hours (processing)
  2. Manual hours (discrepancies)
  3. % automatic reconciliation
  4. Manual hours (parsing new files)
  5. Manual hours (ERP configuration)
- Additional KPIs: response time for new processes, dispute resolution efficiency
