### Ignacio’s Entrepreneurial Journey

- Started MBA at Harvard with goal of launching company
- Spent months ideating without finding conviction in any idea
- Discovered reconciliation problem during summer internship at US fintech
- First-hand experience led to conviction - “there’s something here to do”
- Validated through research, discovery, iteration with design partners and customers
- Never had to pivot - thesis from first MIT pitch remains similar to current roadmap

### Company Overview & Positioning

- Building AI-native reconciliation platform
- Same problem space as Symmetric but different approach:
  - More plug-and-play, self-serve
  - Less consulting and professional services
  - AI-driven vs static SaaS requiring consulting
- Target market: Fintechs and large (non-enterprise) segments
- US-focused from day one vs Symmetric’s LatAm-first approach
- Cost-efficient with team based in Buenos Aires

### Current Traction & Growth

- 5 paying clients currently using platform
- Growing 1-2 new clients per month (varies by month)
- Larger contracts creating variability
- Launched Pre-Seed round in July
- Team: 2 co-founders in NYC, 3rd co-founder with tech team in Buenos Aires

### MBA Value & Recommendations

- Financial ROI likely negative if calculated purely
- Real value in intangibles:
  - Personal experience and lifelong friendships
  - Academic stimulation and elite institution resources
  - Network of ambitious, like-minded people
- Ranking for entrepreneurship: Stanford > MIT > Harvard
- Harvard improving but Stanford “another level” for entrepreneurship
- Cultural differences matter:
  - Harvard: 950 students, \~20 entrepreneurs - majority risk-averse
  - Stanford/MIT: Opposite culture - consulting seen negatively
- Strong recommendation to visit schools 2-3 nights before applying

### Application Strategy & Advice

- Apply first round (September deadline) always better
- GMAT: 700+ required for top schools, diminishing returns above 780
- Application consultant essential ($4k investment worth every penny)
- Storytelling crucial - consultant helps craft narrative schools want to hear
- Many scholarships available for Latinos
- Student loans with subsidized rates viable option
