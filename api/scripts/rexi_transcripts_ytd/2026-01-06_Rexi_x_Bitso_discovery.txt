### AUM Reconciliation Pain Points

- Primary issue: all balance snapshots from different wallets and vaults aren’t synchronized in time
  - Millions of vaults require simultaneous data capture
  - Time lag between first and last balance check creates false discrepancies
  - Most alerts are timing-related, not real problems
- Secondary challenges with provider dependencies:
  - Fireblocks: internal transfers disappear for 1-2 minutes
  - USD movements via USDC: money missing 15-30 minutes during transfers
  - No settlement time events available from providers
  - Missing top-up transactions in ARS (manual console entries not captured)

### Current Internal Tool Assessment

- Transactional reconciliation: \~98% solved, manageable workload
- AUM reconciliation: \~50% performance vs target
- 8-12 person payment ops team handling discrepancies
- Flexibility advantage: can customize for Bitso’s specific provider quirks and internal processes
- ROI considerations: 3-5+ million USD already invested in development

### Bitso’s Resolution Strategy

- Technical improvements planned:
  - Run reconciliation multiple times if discrepancies found
  - Minimize timestamp gaps between balance checks
  - Focus on total balances vs individual wallets for ARS top-ups
- Acknowledged limitations: some provider issues unsolvable without vendor cooperation
- Priority: achieve 95%+ performance on AUM reconciliation
