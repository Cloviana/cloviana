### FiruxPay Overview

- Colombian fintech solving point-of-sale fraud for merchants
  - Prevents fake payment screenshots through real bank transaction verification
  - Uses Prometeo API to read actual money movement between customer and merchant accounts
  - Targeting hotels, restaurants, and retail initially
- Operations across Colombia (Medellín, Bucaramanga, Bogotá)
  - Plans to expand to Mexico and broader Latin America
- MVP launching this week after completing KYC and documentation

### Integration Opportunity

- FiruxPay captures clean transaction data via WhatsApp and tech partners
- Potential to send structured data to Rexi for backend reconciliation
  - Would complement FiruxPay’s fraud prevention with Rexi’s reconciliation capabilities
  - Addresses merchant pain point of month-end transaction reconciliation
- Technical integration feasible via API

### Market Positioning Concerns

- Rexi typically works with banks, payment processors, high-volume clients
- Uncertainty about merchant willingness/ability to pay for Rexi services
- Average transaction sizes in Colombia:
  - \~200,000 pesos for general commerce
  - \~100,000 pesos for service payments
- Need to validate actual customer pain points and pricing tolerance

### Next Steps

- Wait for FiruxPay MVP launch and initial merchant onboarding
- Reconnect once FiruxPay has real customer data to assess:
  - Actual reconciliation needs from merchants
  - Customer experience requirements
  - Willingness to pay and budget ranges
- Maintain contact for future collaboration opportunities
