### Product Demo Overview

- Cashea team evaluating Rexi’s reconciliation platform
- Focus on wallet functionality initially, potential expansion to buy now pay later (BNPL) team
- Platform designed as self-service, plug and play solution
- Full autonomy for Cashea without dependency on Rexi

### Support & Implementation Model

- Extensive hand-holding during initial setup phase
- Close collaboration on functional and technical requirements
- Direct access to founders via WhatsApp/Slack groups
- SaaS model with automatic updates
  - Security improvements
  - New functionality releases
  - Scalability enhancements
- Draft SLA document available if needed

### Pricing Structure Options

- Three flexible models available:
  1. 100% flat fee - monthly fixed cost regardless of volume processed
  2. Hybrid model - fixed platform fee + variable component
    - Variable based on transaction volume
    - Depends on number of reconciliation flows and data sources
  3. 100% variable - scales directly with usage
- Model selection based on client preference for cost predictability vs. growth flexibility

- **Potential mini demo**: Introduction to FinOps team or BNPL team with dummy data
- **Regulatory considerations**: Cashea waiting on superintendency license approval
  - Launch potentially delayed from April to May/June
  - Variable pricing model preferred due to regulatory uncertainty
- **Internal approval**: Cashea to review proposal with C-level leadership
