- Two disbursement rails:

  2. Traditional rail: Work with aggregator partners who provide FX rates and handle disbursements
- Multiple reconciliation nodes: pay-in, conversion, and pay-out
- Has existing semi-automatic reconciliations with Intermex through SQL scripts in BigQuery
- Previously spoke with Simetrik 6-7 months ago but engineering team pushed back, claiming 70% of integration effort would be data transformation
