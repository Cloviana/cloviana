### Operational Reality & Discrepancy Resolution

- Symmetric identifies matches/mismatches but doesn’t resolve discrepancies

  - Amount differences
  - Missing transactions (either side)
  - Timing mismatches
- Country teams spend days resolving discrepancies manually
- Common issues found:
  - Transactions approved by dLocal but rejected by processor
  - Network failures (Visa, bank connections, system updates)
  - Cross-border processing complications
