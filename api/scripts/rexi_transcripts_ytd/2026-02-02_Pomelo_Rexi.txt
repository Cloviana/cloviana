### Current Payment Process & Pain Points

- Pomelo depends on client good faith for payment reporting
  - Client reports payment in dashboard
  - Pomelo validates and approves without seeing bank confirmation
  - Gap of 1-2 days before bank verification (up to 24 hours in Colombia)
- Manual reconciliation process
  - Download payment reports and bank extracts from SAP via Interbanking
  - Cross-reference using Excel spreadsheet with manual tagging
  - Junior staff member handles daily operations
- Risk of service cuts to paying clients
  - “Half the time when we inform a cut, they say ‘hey, I already paid’”
  - Cannot implement automatic service cuts due to payment confirmation delays
  - Large clients (Santander, Dollar App, Union) don’t report payments at all

### Proposed Rexi Solution

- Direct bank API integration to eliminate client reporting dependency
  - Connect to bank APIs with Pomelo’s corporate credentials
  - Real-time payment ingestion and automatic reconciliation
  - Tolerance settings for amount variations (0.05% flexibility)
- Automated conciliation workflow
  1. Bank movements feed into Rexi platform
  2. Match against existing payment reports in Pomelo database
  3. Generate notifications only when action required
  4. Export results to Pomelo back office or send via Slack/email
- Problem resolution module
  - Configurable rules for handling discrepancies
  - Dashboard for operations team to manage exceptions

### Technical Implementation Plan

- Bank integration priority by feasibility
  1. Mexico (BBVA) - highest volume, most reliable banking system
  2. Argentina
  3. Peru
  4. Colombia (lowest priority due to 24-hour delays)
- Required connections
  - Bank APIs for transaction data (primary approach)
  - Pomelo payment reports database (read-only access)
  - Back office system for automated confirmations
- Fallback option: Interbanking integration if direct bank APIs unavailable

### Business Impact & Justification

- Project objectives
  1. Eliminate financial cost of inadvertent client financing
  2. Prevent unnecessary service cuts to paying clients
  3. Enable real-time operations instead of T+1 processing
- Priority level: Q2 target, not urgent but strategically necessary
- Customer experience driver: Foncho (new CSM lead from Mastercard) emphasized large clients won’t use dashboard for payment reporting
- Budget approval path: Lucas → Director of Operations (Gilmar) → COO
