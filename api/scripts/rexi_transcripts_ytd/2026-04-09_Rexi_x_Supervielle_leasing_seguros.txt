### Meeting Overview

- Pilot project discussion for leasing and insurance automation with Supervielle Bank
- Three main pain points identified for Rexi automation
- Focus on processes requiring no systems integration for rapid deployment
- Goal: prioritize initiatives for proof of concept with Eduardo’s approval

### Insurance Process - Deceased Policyholders (Jimena)

- **Process Overview:**
  - Life insurance for retirees aged 65-92 covering credit cards, loans, and advances
  - Monthly reconciliation of insured amounts vs. company payments
  - Coverage capped at 5 million ARS per client
- **Current Manual Process:**
  1. Receive monthly insured client base from three products
  2. Send deceased client list to insurance company
  3. Company responds with payment amounts
  4. Manual reconciliation of three key elements:
    - Client identification
    - Operation number
    - Payment amount
- **Three Reconciliation Scenarios:**
  1. Perfect match: client owed 10, insured for 10, bank balance shows 10 → automatic cancellation
  2. Underpayment: company pays less than owed → difference goes to loss
  3. Overpayment: company pays more than owed → difference goes to profit
- **Time Investment:**
  - Days 20-30 of each month: 100% dedication (Jimena’s time)
  - Represents 1/3 of Jimena’s monthly workload
- **Risk Example:**
  - Credit card data error nearly caused 400M ARS loss from September to February
  - Only caught through manual analysis by team
- **Timing Issues:**
  - Currently collecting December claims in April
  - Multi-month follow-up required for case management
  - Significant audit exposure

### Leasing Reconciliation (Martín)

- **Core Challenge:**
  - Reconcile three systems: internal leasing system, BanTotal (bank core), and supplier payment details
  - 4-day payment lag creates constant mismatches
- **Current Manual Process:**
  1. Team manually enters expenses in Excel from leasing system
  2. Extract payment reports from Dynamics (supplier payments)
  3. Cross-reference with BanTotal accounting
  4. Color-coding system for tracking (green = reconciled, red = pending)
  5. Manual cut-and-paste operations throughout
- **Volume and Complexity:**
  - Single invoice can contain 500+ line items (one-to-many reconciliation)
  - Multiple suppliers with same invoice numbers require careful matching
  - Credit notes and cancellations add complexity
- **Time Investment:**
  - Minimum 1 hour daily for Martín
  - Additional team members inputting data
  - Estimated 2 hours daily total team effort
  - Weekly 1-hour team meetings to resolve pending cases
- **Identification Keys:**
  - Supplier, invoice number, amount, and date matching required
  - System already has deterministic rules for automation

### Leasing Account Control (Vanessa)

- **Process Description:**
  - Per-contract reconciliation: payment to supplier → accounting entry → client contract charge
  - Each of 800+ annual contracts requires complete accounting verification
- **Current Gaps:**
  1. Manual verification of contract completeness
  2. Missing charges for interests, commissions, and fees
  3. Revenue leakage from incomplete billing
- **Reconciliation Scope:**
  - Account-by-account balance verification
  - Import transactions require multi-stage payments before contract activation
  - Audit requirements for zero-balance account closure
- **Time Investment:**
  - 30 minutes daily when performed
  - Currently done every other day (1 hour per session)
  - Many accounts not reviewed due to capacity constraints
  - Significant unmonitored risk exposure
