### Roger’s Background & Anatomy Financial Overview

- Roger leads financial products at Anatomy Financial
  - Previously head of product at Novo (SMB banking) - grew team from 3 to 8 people
  - Led spend experience at N26 US team
  - Former Bank of America global markets experience
- Anatomy Financial: vertically aligned with healthcare
  - Building a bank for healthcare providers with ACH, RTP, and Fedwire rails
  - Partner bank: Live Oak Bank (largest SBA loan issuer in US, great lending partner)
  - Revenue streams: SaaS (AI-powered EOB conversion) + banking services
  - Current structure: DDA (Direct Deposit Account) model
    - Customers own their accounts directly
    - Uses Finsact as ledger company
    - No end-of-day batch posting required
    - Most reconciliation handled by the bank

### Market Opportunity: FBO Compliance Gap

- Companies acting as PSPs in healthcare are breaking money transmitter license laws
  - Receiving money and passing to another party requires proper licensing
  - Many don’t realize they’re out of compliance
- Solution requires FBO (For Benefit Of) account structure
  - FBO must be for benefit of payor or payee
  - Banks can earn overnight float sitting in the middle
  - Many banks lack FBO infrastructure (6-month build typically)
- Anatomy currently rejects these customers due to DDA-only structure
  - Exclusivity agreement with Live Oak Bank prevents partnering with other banks
  - Live Oak is lead investor, creating additional relationship complexity
