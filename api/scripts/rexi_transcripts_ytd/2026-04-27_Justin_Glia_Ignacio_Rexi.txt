### Sales Strategy for Banks & Credit Unions

- **#1 advice: conferences, but the small ones. Not the big ones**

  - Target tiny regional events (10 banks/credit unions attending)
  - Much cheaper than large conferences like Money 2020
  - Better networking opportunities vs expensive mega events
  - Look for easy-to-reach regional conferences from NYC base

- **#2 outbound (cold-calling works)**

  - Direct outreach remains effective
  - Physical presence crucial - “literally show up with donuts”
