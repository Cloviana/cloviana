### B2B Payment Digitization Trends

- US market shift from cash/checks to digital payments
  - 2019: 50% cash and checks
  - 2024: 32% cash and checks
  - Volume primarily moved to ACH, not cards or real-time payments
- Latin America complexity for merchants scaling across markets
  - Fragmented payment methods by country
  - No unified solution across region
  - Expensive local PSPs with limited control

### Payment Orchestrator Evolution

- Front-end innovation solved acceptance, routing, processing, fraud
- Orchestrators centralize PSPs, gateways, payment methods
- Added value: fraud control, reconciliation automation
- Gap remains in back-end operations after money movement

### Treasury Function Transformation

- Evolution from pure cash management to strategic role
- 50%+ of treasurers now invited to C-suite transformation meetings
- Expanded KPIs include risk management, digitization
- Need for real-time data and automated reconciliation
- Challenge: making decisions on fragmented, delayed, disputed information

### Rexi’s Market Position

- AI-powered financial control layer for complex payment flows
- Sits between processors, banks, PSPs, ledgers
- Creates single source of operational truth
- Focus: back-end data orchestration, not money handling
- Target: banks, processors, insurance companies, marketplaces
- Serves 100 transactions/day to 50M transactions/day range

### Technology and Implementation

- Connects via API, SFTP, CSV downloads, PDF emails
- AI learns specific workflows and business complexity
- Three core offerings:
  1. Automated reconciliation across all flows
  2. Real-time financial visibility across systems
  3. Agentic workflows on reconciled data layer
- Operates in both US and Latin America markets
- Same data orchestration challenges globally

### Market Maturity Insights

- US and Latin America reconciliation maturity surprisingly similar
- Financial services focus due to highest transaction volumes
- Competitive landscape includes BlackLine, Trintech, Ledge, Archon, Duco, Symmetric

### AI and Banking Perspective

- Banks initially scared of AI adoption due to security concerns
- Controlled AI within specific, measurable fields more acceptable
