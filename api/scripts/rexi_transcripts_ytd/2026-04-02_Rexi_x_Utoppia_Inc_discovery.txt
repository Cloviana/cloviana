### Unified Balance Product (in testing)

- New product: “Unified Balance” — card-centric model replacing checking account-centric model
  - Customer sees a single consolidated balance, always on the card
  - Card remains prepaid in the backend; frontend abstracts this entirely
- Money-in flow: ACH received → automatically loaded onto prepaid card
- Money-out flow: unload from card → withdrawal executed
  - Movements are not instantaneous, so a liquidity account bridges the gap
  - Example: $100 bill payment → immediate debit from liquidity account → bill paid → card unload arrives at T+1 to replenish
- Currently in testing with Stefano and one other person; not yet live
- Two reconciliation layers this creates:
  1. Liquidity account: monitor every debit/credit and match to corresponding transaction + confirm card unload replenishment
  2. Card-level: loads and unloads against card transactions
- Genius data team trying to build something internally, but Nicole doesn’t want to depend on that
- Future scope: rewards/points system → each point issued = a liability on the books → adds a third reconciliation layer (accounting-level)
