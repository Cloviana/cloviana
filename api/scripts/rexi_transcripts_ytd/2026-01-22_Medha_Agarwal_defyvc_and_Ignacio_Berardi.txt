### Product & Technical Developments

- Enhanced platform capabilities
  - Can now handle high-volume processing (40M+ transactions)
  - Improved architecture for enterprise-scale customers
- Accounting/treasury integration focus
  - Customers consistently request finance workflow integration
  - Building home-grown model around accounting system integrations
  - 2 customers actively pushing for this capability

### Business Model Experimentation

- New managed deployment approach
  - $1M upfront for 9-month full implementation and configuration
  - Customer owns infrastructure after deployment
  - Low retainer for ongoing support on delivered components
  - Addresses customer concerns about vendor lock-in and pricing power
- Motivation from competitive landscape
  - Incumbents (BlackLine, others) charge $2-3M annually
  - High switching costs create pricing leverage for existing providers
  - Privacy/security concerns with SaaS deployments

### Market Position & Strategy

- Current customer base spans multiple verticals
  - Banks, payment processors, insurance companies, fintech
  - Sees need to focus on specific niche for efficient scaling
- Q1 priority: Define ideal customer segment
  - Identify where product-market fit is strongest
  - Focus distribution and product development efforts
  - Current broad approach feels inefficient

### Next Steps

- Fundraising timeline: Mid-2025 based on experiment results
  - Want organic approach based on capital needs, not hype
  - Current burn rate provides flexibility due to cost-efficient Argentina team
