### Vizum Business Model & Pain Points

- Cross-border payments using “stablecoin sandwich” method
  - Convert Mexican pesos → USDT → destination currency
  - Faster and cheaper than traditional methods
- Client base: importers, exporters, fintechs, casinos, trading companies
  - Distributed across various industries
- Current reconciliation process entirely manual
  - Excel-based across 8 different partners
  - Multiple account types: concentrator accounts, direct client subaccounts
  - Daily process takes \~40 minutes for CFO Gonzalo
  - Different fee structures (0.2% to 2%) per client complicate tracking

### Current Reconciliation Challenges

- Manual balance checking across all partner accounts
- Complex pay-in/payout flows with different exchange rates
- Recent issues with untracked refunds from partners
  - No major losses but time-intensive error hunting
  - Example: Missing refund required partner investigation
- Existing solutions too institutional with “700 tabs”
  - Require months of integration for full team
  - Don’t fit mid-market needs

### Technical Infrastructure

- 4-5 partner connections for reconciliation
- Some partners fully API-integrated, others in progress
- No centralized data lake yet
- Currently extracting data as needed from each partner

### Rexi Solution Fit

- AI-native, configurable platform
- Transaction-level reconciliation vs. balance-level
- Automated root cause clustering with approval workflow
- Learning system that codifies approved resolutions
- Workflow-based pricing (not transaction volume)
  - Fixed 12-month contracts
  - Assessment based on volume, complexity, connectors
