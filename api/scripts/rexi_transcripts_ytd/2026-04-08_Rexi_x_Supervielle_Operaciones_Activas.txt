### Control Contable Process Overview

- Cobranzas de préstamos SIOC (jubilados) through sistema AS
- Rubros transitorios en Bantotal (3 entries) where differences impact when cobranzas fail
- Current process: macro por sucursal to identify differences, then manual contabilization
- Information granularity varies by rubro:
  - Some rubros: only sucursal data
  - Others: sucursal + número de cliente + número de operación
- Data sources (archivos crudos):
  1. Sistema AS (IBM petitions system)
    - Detalle diferencias with apertura by préstamo number, sucursal, amount, user
  2. Bantotal (IBM petitions system)
    - Saldos diarios (daily balances)
    - Saldos totales (total balances)

### Current Workflow and Pain Points

- Daily process (always previous day, Monday includes weekend activity)
- Macro processing steps:
  1. Download raw files from both systems
  2. Group differences by sucursal
  3. Create daily and sucursal breakdown
  4. Drill into detalle diferencias to identify specific failed operations
- Manual resolution options:
  - Roxana regularizes directly in Bantotal or SIOC
  - Inform user who made the cobranza to correct
- Additional complexity: Pago Fácil cobranzas
  - Daily control through separate petitions
  - Cross-reference with daily email from Pago Fácil
  - Minimal differences but handle rejections due to insufficient saldos
  - Generate additional asientos contables for reconciliation

### Impact Assessment

- Time commitment: 2-3 hours daily (depends on difference volume)
  - Investigation phase consumes most time
  - Requires coordination with recupero team and sucursales
  - Case-by-case analysis required
- Risk factors:
  - Customer complaints from incorrect cobranzas
  - Automatic mora generation for customers when cuotas don’t impact properly
  - Refinancing errors requiring immediate resolution
- Dependencies:
  - Team depends on users for external regularizations
  - Not blocking other areas
- Example scenarios: préstamo cobrado in SIOC but failed to impact Bantotal due to system errors

### Deprioritized Initiative

- Préstamo con código de descuento
  - Preventive automation for unreleased product
  - Cannot pilot until product launches and data flow established
