SUMMARY
============================================================
### Their background

- Pollinate has built a platform connecting banks with payment processors through three core products:
  - Onboarding for merchant acquisition
  - Portal for transaction management (already offers reconciliation capabilities)
  - Marketplace for third-party integrations
- Currently operational with all merchants on their platform, including Truth’s merchant portfolio
- Sam Yates serves as Growth Advisor at Synapto and is leading the marketplace RFI process
- Target customers are primarily SMBs with high street presence: corner stores, coffee shops, barbers
- Merchants typically take in-person payments with some online capabilities
- Portal shows real-time transactions, payouts per store, charges, adjustments, and final settlement amounts

### Pain points and needs

- Low merchant engagement within the portal is their primary challenge
  - “Our problem isn’t reconciliation. Our problem is engagement within the portal”
  - Variable uptake on existing reconciliation features
  - Many SMB merchants only reconcile every few months or at tax year end, not daily
- Current reconciliation capability is basic:
  - Balance-level matching only, not individual transaction matching
  - “It’s comparing balances. Yeah. Got it”
  - Simple view: “Here are your transactions. Here’s what’s settled. Here are your chargebacks. Here are the disputes”
- Need to drive more merchant engagement post-onboarding
  - Looking at marketplace as solution to engage merchants with wider ecosystem
  - Seeking products and services that banks don’t currently offer
- Merchants lack visibility into whether they’re receiving correct settlement amounts
  - “Merchants have no idea if they’re getting the right amount they should be getting”

### Questions or concerns

- Uncertainty about value proposition above their current capabilities
