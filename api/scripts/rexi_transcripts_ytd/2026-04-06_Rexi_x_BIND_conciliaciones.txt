### Laura’s Use Case: PSP & Current Accounts

- Laura manages a group of affiliated companies; largest is a PSP (payment processor)
- PSP has \~200 current accounts (+ sub-accounts) with the bank
  - Each sub-account holds different clients and different products
- Reconciliation need: operational data vs. accounting data
  - “What I see in the bank vs. what I see reflected in accounting”
- Accounting system: Tango (ERP)
- Bank-side data: a BIND transaction file (generic movement codes, no unique identifier)
- No unique reconciliation key — matching requires creative combination of date, time, amount, CUIT, etc.
- Matching logic: 1:1
- Currently not 100% reconciled — significant open pain point
