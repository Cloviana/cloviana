### Their background

- RecargaPay is a 20-year-old company that has evolved through multiple business pivots
  - Started with phone cards, then moved to online cell phone recharges with credit cards
  - Similar evolution path to companies like M-Pesa in Kenya or bKash in Bangladesh
  - Now operates as a neo-bank with full banking services despite the name “Recarga”
- Current business model includes deposits, cards, bill payments, and loans
  - Operates with combination of licenses rather than single banking license
  - Serves approximately 1.2 million unique customers per month
  - Each customer generates 7-10 transactions on average
- Joaquín Otaño comes from [avenida.com](http://avenida.com) background (10+ years ago experience with credit card processing)
  - Currently reports directly to Gustavo (COO and company founder)
  - CFO is leaving and COO will temporarily take over finance leadership
  - New CFO likely won’t manage fin ops - will stay under operations

### Pain points and needs

- Reconciliation volume and complexity is massive challenge
  - Processing 20-25 million transactions per month across multiple products
  - Each transaction requires multiple reconciliations (against payment, provider, service)
  - Current process involves 4 full-time employees working daily reconciliations
- Current systems (Simetrik and Equalz) have significant limitations
  - Mix of daily automated ingestion and manual processes requiring portal downloads
  - “Simetrik is a difficult system to configure”
  - No real-time alerts - issues discovered hours later at 3 PM instead of morning
  - Format changes break entire reconciliation flows until manually remapped
- Reconciliation challenges across multiple product lines:
  1. PIX payments (with BTG as provider)
  2. Credit card payments via platform (with acquirers)
  3. Credit card issuing (Mastercard network via Bismo)
  4. Bill payments and services
  5. Internal wallet reconciliation
- Key operational pain points identified:
  - Manual data ingestion processes
  - Time-intensive discrepancy resolution
  - Lack of automatic alerts and reporting
  - System fragility when providers change file formats
- Credit card reconciliation particularly problematic
  - “Credit card companies don’t want you to reconcile - it generates rework, disputes, etc.”
  - Industry-wide challenge that creates market opportunity

### Questions or concerns

- Expressed skepticism about existing providers adding AI capabilities
  - “All providers want to increase prices by 15% because they’re incorporating AI”
  - “Your product as it is, I don’t need anything more, don’t need summaries”
  - Concerned about vendors lying about AI capabilities just for price increases
- Worried about building internal AI/automation team
  - “If you do it yourself, you don’t learn from the market or system”
  - “Need 5 people just for reconciliation automation when goal is to reduce headcount”
- Wants to avoid adding another layer to existing system cascade
  - Solution needs to replace, not supplement current tools
  - Must cover everything currently handled by existing providers

### Budget and timeline

- Current spending: Approximately $120,000 annually on Simetrik and Equalz combined
  - Considers these “not among RecargaPay’s most expensive contracts”
  - No attachment to existing providers - willing to switch for better solution
- Budget philosophy: “Any change that saves us FTEs and increases efficiency and security is worth making”
- Timeline urgency: High priority for Q2 implementation
  - “This is a need from last year - we’re behind, just haven’t found the solution”
  - Willing to prioritize if solution is truly plug-and-play
  - COO (founder) is “obsessed with optimizing operations” and driving 2026 automation initiatives
- Scaling concerns driving urgency
  - Credit card penetration expected to 5x-6x transaction volume with same user base
  - Cannot scale current 4-person reconciliation team proportionally
