### Rexi Overview and Problem Space

- Founded during Harvard MBA using school resources and platform
- Discovered reconciliation pain point during summer internship at Comune (NYC fintech)
  - Chief of staff role involved finance workflows
  - Experienced reconciliation complexity firsthand
- Core problem: Fintech companies without banking licenses operate with partner banks
  - Creates layered architecture requiring reconciliation between software layer and actual bank movements
  - Current solutions either expensive enterprise-only or manual patchwork approaches

### AI Strategy and Business Model Innovation

- Two-pronged AI approach:
  1. Product scalability: Tool feels like building custom internal software
    - Encompasses different setups without professional services overhead
  2. Business model disruption: Automate what competitors charge for
    - Incumbents charge $500K-$1M+ annually due to professional services costs
    - Target non-consumption market excluded by high prices
- AI agents replace account managers and forward deployed engineers
  - Goal: Never need account managers (“if we have an account manager, we failed”)

### Target Market and Customer Segments

- Current targets: Sponsor banks, payment providers, fintech platforms, payment orchestrators, insurance companies
- Ideal customer profile still refining:
  - Multiple payment flows (increases complexity)
  - High transaction volume (makes ROI worthwhile)
- Performance indicators achieved:
  - 80% reduction in manual work
  - Risk reduction and write-off savings
  - Intentionally keeping 20% human oversight for sensitive workflow

### Platform Architecture and Roadmap

- Five-agent system:
  1. Ingestion agent: Pulls data from sources
  2. Transformation agent: Standardizes formats
  3. Processing agent: Matches transactions, finds patterns
  4. Discrepancy resolution: AI-powered investigation
  5. Coordination agent: User interface and workflow management
- Key design principles: Plug-and-play, self-serve, compliance-ready
- Roadmap priorities:
  - Dynamic integrator for instant API connections
  - Embedded solutions for platform distribution
  - Collaboration workflows for network effects (sponsor banks can push adoption to fintech partners)

### Sales Challenges and Solutions

- Sensitive automation conversation: Selling to teams being automated
  - Approach 1: Brief budget approver about sensitivity before user conversations
  - Approach 2: Reframe as higher-value work enablement vs job elimination
  - Focus on investigating more discrepancies, reducing write-offs
- Additional resistance from tech teams claiming they could build internally
  - Counter with resource allocation argument: focus engineering on core competencies
