### Rexi Product Overview & Vision

- Current product: AI-powered reconciliation platform for banks, payment companies, insurance companies, and marketplaces
  - Connects to data sources (statements, ledgers, payment processors)
  - Automates transaction matching and discrepancy resolution
  - Handles reporting, dashboards, and accounting entries
- Dynamic integrator capability
  - Builds custom integrations for each client automatically
  - Creates API connections, SFTP, CSV downloads, PDF email parsing
  - Eliminates need for pre-built integration library
- Long-term vision: Become “cloud code for fin ops”
  - Start with reconciliations as wedge product
  - Expand to other finance/operations automation (accounting, treasury, reporting, accounts receivable)
  - Target CFOs and COOs as primary platform users

### Go-to-Market Strategy: Partnership Distribution

- Armar el happy path approach:
  1. Create list of 50-100 target companies that must be closed
  2. If anything deviates from expected process, understand exactly why
  3. Learn from deviations and prevent repetition
- Partnership distribution as key growth lever
  - Find providers that serve your ICP and contain many potential customers
  - Offer revenue sharing agreements rather than direct sales
  - Partners benefit from upsell opportunities without product development investment
- Partner value proposition requirements:
  1. Product too complex for partner to build internally
  2. Partner lacks necessary talent/expertise
  3. Not partner’s core business focus
  4. Partner doesn’t want liability risk of signing off on reconciliations
- Integration benefits for end customers
  - Improves user experience significantly
  - Simplifies Rexi onboarding process
  - Reduces biggest B2B pain point: system integrations

### Partnership Execution & Risks

- Reaching potential partners:
  - Target Director of Partnerships roles - they always respond
  - Large companies typically have someone handling partnership deals
  - Much easier outreach than direct customer acquisition
- Partnership sales requirements:
  - Different deck and funnel than direct sales
  - Separate value proposition focused on partner benefits
  - Revenue sharing model where partner earns without direct billing
- Risk management:
  - Partners may eventually copy or cut you out
  - Stay close to potential competitors in their roadmap
  - Swim fast to build moat before they can replicate
  - Use competitive pressure as roadmap clarity tool
