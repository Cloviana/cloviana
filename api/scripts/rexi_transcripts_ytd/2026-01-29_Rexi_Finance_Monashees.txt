### Market Thesis & Timing

- Core mission: Money shouldn’t get lost in financial systems (Synapse collapse as wake-up call)
  - $27M publicly lost in Synapse, affecting most vulnerable populations served by FinTech
- Financial services modularization created reconciliation complexity
  - Pendulum swinging from aggregation → modularization → need for guardrails
  - FinTech 3.0 (stablecoins, crypto, blockchain) will amplify transaction volume/complexity
- AI timing advantage over previous solutions:
  - Proper Finance (YC, sold to QuickBooks) faced technical limitations 6-7 years ago
  - Kyle Maloney (Proper founder) now angel investor in Rexi
  - Current AI capabilities solve problems that blocked earlier attempts

### Go-to-Market & Defensibility Insights

- Strong conviction maintained from initial hypothesis (October 2024 notes similar to today)
- Design partner → paying customer conversion validates market pull
- Internal build unlikely for most companies:
  - Complex technical product requiring specialized knowledge
  - Non-core competency for most businesses (except payment processors like Tapi)
  - Companies will automate enabling functions with AI agents, focus resources on core value
- Competitive landscape: Symmetric serves one segment well, legacy players (BlackLine, Trentech) focus on banking
  - Rest of market highly fragmented with no dominant solution
