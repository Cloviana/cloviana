  - Blockchain exposes full transaction history and wallet contents
- SKALE building native programmable privacy
  - Private payments and tokens
  - Encrypted data directly in Solidity
  - Compliant programmable privacy (not full anonymity like Tornado Cash)

### Neobank Challenges & Opportunities

- Current neobank pain points:

  - Private checking/savings accounts on-chain
  - Encrypted deposits and transfers
  - Custom compliance triggers and rebalancing
