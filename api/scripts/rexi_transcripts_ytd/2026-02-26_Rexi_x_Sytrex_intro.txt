### Sytrex Business Model & Pricing

- Análisis de volumetría approach
  - Based on number of submissions received
  - Considers size/complexity of submissions (number of files, etc.)
- Creates plan covering 1-2 business lines initially
  - Land with low friction, expand as clients gain confidence
  - Ticket size increases as they expand to more organizational areas
- Pricing methodology:
  1. Calculate time needed to process submissions
  2. Benchmark cost of people’s time
  3. Factor in opportunity cost in terms of hit rate
  4. Translate all variables to monetary value
  5. Charge approximately one-third of total value created
- Automated proposal process
  - Sends 5-question Typeform
  - Can deliver economic proposal within one hour
- Variable pricing by number of submissions
  - Plan covers up to certain threshold
  - Additional charges if volume significantly exceeds (50%+ or double)

### Go-to-Market Strategy

- Multi-channel approach evolution:
  1. Network of known contacts (first layer of clients)
  2. Snowball effect with high NPS - clients refer to other clients, brokers, MGAs
  3. Partnerships with MGA incubators
- Conference-driven sales essential
  - “Insurance impossible to crack without conferences”
  - ITC in Vegas (October) - primary event with 80,000 attendees
    - Creates hitlist for 1:1 meetings
    - Side events more valuable than main conference
    - Natural relationship building through client introductions to dinners/events
- Geographic expansion strategy
  - Consolidated LATAM first for ongoing revenue base
  - Allows venture capital for experimentation vs. payroll
  - Now expanding to Middle East, Asia (Dubai trip in 2 months)
  - London market attractive
  - US remains priority #1

### Insurance Payment Reconciliation Landscape

- Pain point less acute in insurance vs. fintech
  - Insurance operations more straightforward with single payment providers
- Payment moments in insurance:
  1. **Placement phase** - receive payment, issue policy
    - Retail: gateway, payment links, or traditional wiring
    - Reinsurance (large deals): 100% wire transfers ($250k+ premiums)
  2. **Monthly subscriptions** (retail-focused)
    - Auto, property insurance
    - Giants like Geico already automated end-to-end
    - Market leaders have extensive automation
  3. **Claims processing**
    - Adjusters (third-party services) receive and evaluate claims
    - Review limits, coverage, deductibles, approve/deny portions
    - Reconciliation: adjuster verdict vs. insurer reserves to be released
    - Example: 15 stolen phones claimed, adjuster approves 12 (3 deemed fraudulent)
