### Pain points and needs

**Monthly Closing Process:**

- Monthly insurance company closing process creates significant complications each month
- Chile operation with MetLife represents their biggest monthly closing challenge

**Three Categories of Accounting Problems:**

1. **Accounting Entry Generation (Type 1 Problem):**
  - Complex business rules for determining premium recognition and profit recognition timing
  - Different products require different accounting treatments
2. **Interface with Insurance Companies (Type 2 Problem):**
  - Currently work with file-based interfaces but considering API integration
  - Issues transcribing well-generated entries to insurance company systems
3. **Data Coherence (Type 3 Problem):**
  - Most complex issue requiring manual work
  - Inconsistencies between operational data and accounting entries
  - Example: “If I say I provisioned premiums I issued and collected 100%, the ledger should match, but sometimes doesn’t coincide”

**Technical Challenges:**

- Need to cross-reference multiple data sources (up to 5 databases) to generate single accounting entry

- Different business logic and actuarial requirements across products
- Current system generates entries but comparison against operational data reveals discrepancies

**Specific Use Cases Mentioned:**

- Premium provisioning entries require checking policy status and payment status across multiple databases

- Need to validate that accounting entries match operational reality across policy sales, collections, claims, and cancellations
