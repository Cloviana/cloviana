### Pollinate Overview & Platform Capabilities

- Joseph Cox: Product Manager at Pollinate (8 years experience)
- Dashboard platform with mini-apps for merchant services
  - Enables merchants to view transactions, invoices, settlements
  - Account management functionality
- B2B2B model: Sell platform to banks who operationalize and resell to customers
- Current development focus:
  - Self-service and assisted onboarding products
  - Marketplace integration for additional revenue streams
  - Fast credit offerings for customers

### Rexi Demo & Technical Capabilities

- Automates reconciliation for high-volume, high-complexity, regulated processes
- Key features demonstrated:
  - Multi-source data ingestion and standardization
  - Two-sweep matching process (ID-based, then merchant ID + card digits + date + amount)
  - Tolerance settings for dates and amounts
  - Critical comparison checks (fees, settlement dates, status)
  - Exception management and manual matching
  - Automated journal entry creation
  - Custom dashboards and reporting
  - Slack/email alerts
