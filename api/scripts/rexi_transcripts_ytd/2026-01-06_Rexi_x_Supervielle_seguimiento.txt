### Pricing Model Discussion

- Current Rexi proposal unviable for Supervielle
  - Base operational cost: $5K/month + $1K per flow
  - Variable transaction costs on top of fixed fees
  - 30+ existing flows would cost $35K+ monthly maintenance alone
  - Additional 30 flows projected, making total costs prohibitive
- Volume scale concerns
  - 1.16M master records + 2.24M detailed records per month
  - Same base data crosses multiple processes, multiplying transaction costs
  - Current manual processes handle 30 flows via Control M and DTF

### Business Model Mismatch

- Supervielle’s expectations vs. Rexi’s offering
  - Expected tool purchase + customization capability (like previous vendors)
  - Previous tools: $500/month maintenance with fixed development costs
  - Rexi’s recurring SaaS model creates vendor lock-in concerns
- Budget dependency issues
  - 2026 budget available but 2027 approval uncertain
  - If budget fails, all development work lost since hosted on Rexi’s cloud
  - Supervielle prefers in-house development to avoid eternal budget dependency

### Integration Requirements

- Rexi’s standard model
  - 120 hours/month: 2 engineers + tech lead + product manager
  - 2 flows per quarter standard delivery
  - Implementation: $9,750 discounted MVP, then operational costs
- Supervielle’s complexity
  - 30 current automated flows for Central Bank compliance
  - 24 via Control M, 6 via DTF
  - Additional 30+ flows identified for automation

### Potential Solutions

- Rexi considering alternative approaches
  - On-premise installation in Supervielle’s cloud (not standard offering)
  - Consultancy model instead of SaaS recurring fees
  - One-year engagement with tool handover
- Supervielle’s preferences
  - Tool remains within bank infrastructure
  - Minimal or no ongoing maintenance costs
  - Consultant-based development vs. subscription service
