-   Current performance: ~0.04% pending transactions, very low losses

### Key Pain Points & Requirements

-   Manual exception analysis consumes most time
-   Scaling challenges with new products (insurance, loans)
-   Knowledge concentration risk - specialists hold critical business logic
-   Need for automatic data export to internal systems (Amazon backend/Databricks)
-   Lack of scalable processes for new product additions
-   FlowCast system for account reconciliation provides minimal value despite cost

-   Real-time vs expired transaction classification
-   Automated alerts and notifications (email/Slack)
