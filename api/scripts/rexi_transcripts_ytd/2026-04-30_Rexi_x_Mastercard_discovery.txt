### Mastercard’s Payment Models & Reconciliation Challenges

- Two distinct operational models:
  1. Post-funded (traditional card rails)
    - Uses SAM system (ACH for cards) with net-zero settlements
    - Generates reconciliation files for charges, debits, returns
  2. Pre-funded (cross-border services)
    - Banks deposit money upfront into virtual accounts
    - Uses non-bank infrastructure for money collection
- Current pain points with reporting formats:
  - Heavy reliance on EDI standards that banks struggle with
  - Clients expect MT940 format but only receive EDI
  - Creates integration challenges and project delays
  - Banks need custom development to convert EDI to preferred formats
