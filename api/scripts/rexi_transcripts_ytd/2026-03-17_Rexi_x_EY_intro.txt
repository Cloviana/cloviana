### EY Partnership Model

- Florencia works with payment solutions generally
- EY collaborates through two models:
  - Subcontracting (most common) - EY pays vendor, client pays EY
  - Joint partnerships - going to market together
- Long approval process required for any alliance
- Currently works with various payment tools/providers

### Rexi Solution Overview

- Automates end-to-end reconciliation process for financial services
- Connects directly to raw data sources (banks, payment processors, internal ledgers)
  - No preprocessing required
  - Maintains audit trail with original files
- AI agent normalizes different file formats and nomenclatures
- Automated matching logic plus AI pattern recognition
- Investigation module for discrepancy resolution with clustering technology
- Self-learning system builds company-specific policies over time

### Target Market & Current Clients

- Primary focus: Financial services, banks, fintechs, PSPs
- Expanding into marketplaces and ecommerce
- Current clients include:
  - Argentina: Superbiel, Despegar, Cachea, Buenbit
  - US: Común, PayLliance, Dartbank
  - In discussions: Cleanver (insurance), HDI Seguros

### Pricing & Implementation

- Fixed 12-month contracts instead of per-transaction pricing
  - Provides cost predictability for growing companies
  - Volume discounts as usage scales
- Implementation timeline: weeks to 1 month (vs competitors’ 3+ months)
- Reconciliation positioned as “loss leader” to expand into broader FinOps platform
- SOC 2 Type 2 certified, pursuing SOC 1 Type 2 and ISO 27001

- Demo available upon request
- Florencia interested in recommending solution to existing clients
