  - Even with complete data universe, cancellations never reach high reconciliation percentages
  - Oracle’s current merchant rev sweep (first pass) shows unreliable cancellation data

  - 50K lines processed every 30 minutes

  - 2 hours manual reconciliation effort

  - 83 hours manual reconciliation effort due to frequent new supplier onboarding
