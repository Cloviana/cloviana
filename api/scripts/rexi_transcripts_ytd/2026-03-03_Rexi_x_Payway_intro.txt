### Payway Overview & Current Conciliation Process

- Franco Dorsa: Product owner of conciliation flow (digital portal)
- Premium clients connect via SFTP to receive reports
  - Other clients download directly from portal
- Payway acts as acquirer but lacks banking access
  - Facilitates information delivery to merchants
  - Merchants handle conciliation with banks/ERPs independently
- API project underway to improve data availability

### Client Pain Points & Market Reality

- Conciliation represents significant client pain point
- Many merchants lack technical capability
  - Don’t understand SFTP basics
  - Hire third-party conciliators (Nubceo, Conciliac, Simetrik)
- Current integrators handle full process: data extraction, bank matching, ERP integration

### Partnership Exploration Options

- Two integration approaches discussed:
  1. Reseller model: Click-through from Payway portal to Rexi
    - Pre-built Payway connector
    - Discounted pricing for Payway clients
    - User manages experience in Rexi platform
  2. White-label solution: API integration within Payway portal
    - Rexi engine processes conciliation backend
    - Results displayed in Payway dashboard
    - Seamless user experience
- Hybrid approaches possible between these extremes

### Competitive Landscape & Decision Making

- Existing conciliators already working with Payway merchants
  - Conciliac, Simetrik mentioned as active players
- Partnership decisions handled by:
  - Head of Product (potential)
  - Partnerships team
  - C-level strategy role
- Franco will investigate proper decision-maker contact

  - Present meeting outcomes and partnership options
  - Determine internal interest and decision pathway
