### Strategic Framework for Growth

- Qualifying prospects effectively
  - How important is the problem you’re solving in the list of other problems they have?
  - Value proposition: how much better your solution is vs the current way they are solving the pain
- Messaging alignment
  - How good are you articulating the solution to the right person in the organization
  - Great ICP + Great Value + Great message - everything should be in the same line
  - If misaligned, creates friction in middle of sales process

### Focus and Positioning Strategy

- ICP selection approach
  1. Ideal scenario: choose ICP
  2. Second best: find ICP
  3. Third best: experimenting
- Benefits of industry focus
  - There is a benefit of knowing the language and knowing the people
  - Create curiosity: People like case studies - show me how the product works great for my competitor
  - Credibility: Once you sell to 3 healthcare companies, it’s easier to sell to others
  - Rumor mill effect amplifies reputation within vertical
- Recommendation: Focus on 10 leads maximum
  - Deep research and preparation for each
  - Learn conversion patterns before scaling
  - Example: AI agent created synthetic database with prospect’s actual data for demo

### Key Insights and Next Steps

- Technical DNA consideration
  - Very technical fintech companies harder to sell to - they have DNA to build internally
  - Better targets: marketplaces, companies where reconciliation isn’t core competency
