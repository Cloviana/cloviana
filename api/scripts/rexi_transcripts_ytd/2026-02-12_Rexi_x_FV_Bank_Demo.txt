### Pain points and needs

- BlackLine decimal place limitations with stablecoins and crypto transactions
- Complex data preparation requirements before using BlackLine
  - “BlackLine basically forces you guys to dump the data in a certain format in a certain way. That requires a lot of work on your side of data preparation”
- Returns and reversals management challenges
  - Each provider manages returns/reversals with different logic, making unified reconciliation difficult
- Rule creation and maintenance difficulties in BlackLine
  - Rules break frequently and require constant updates and adaptations
- High volume of unmatched discrepancies requiring manual investigation
- Matching challenges when transaction IDs are unavailable
- **Additional pain identified: BlackLine’s rigid unmatching process**
  - “Once it’s matched, then it’s tough to… the whole process of unmatching it, and then reloading stuff”
  - “You’ll have to unmatch the entire \[group\] rather than just for that transaction”
  - When editing any part of a matched transaction, must unmatch all related transactions, fix issues, then re-match everything
- **Balance reconciliation workflow issues**
  - Currently must manually upload balances to BlackLine
  - System doesn’t use previous day’s calculated balance as starting point for next day
  - “You have to upload it again… It doesn’t use the balance already obtained to continue”
- Need for approval workflows and audit trails for compliance
- Custody reconciliation complications due to digit handling requirements
