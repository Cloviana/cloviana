### Insurance Industry Structure

- Two core entities in insurance data schemas:
  - La póliza (policy): subscription-based contract with payments
    - Similar to payments world Rexi already handles
  - El siniestro (claims): separate entity with distinct processes/logic
- Good entry point for Rexi to learn insurance vertical
- Opportunity to leverage existing payments expertise

### Pricing Discussion & Models

- 3-month proof of concept: $1,050/month
  - 1.5 months implementation + 1.5 months live testing
  - Start with single use case (MetLife Chile or similar)
- Annual contract options:
  1. Fixed model: predictable monthly cost based on volume estimates
  2. Hybrid model: base platform fee + variable component
  3. 100% variable: scales with transaction volume
- Annual estimate: $25-50K with early customer discount
- Octavio prefers starting fixed, then expanding to other business units

### Competitive Landscape

- Symmetric identified as major competitor
  - Colombian company serving large clients (Rappi, Guala, MercadoPago)
  - Charges millions annually with 100% variable pricing
  - Creates vendor lock-in due to sensitive workflows
  - Guala renewed then prioritized internal development due to $1.5M annual cost
- Rexi’s differentiation: lower cost + pricing predictability without lock-in
