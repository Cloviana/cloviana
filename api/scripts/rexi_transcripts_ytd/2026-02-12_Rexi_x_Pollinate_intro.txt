### Their background

- Pollinate provides AI-powered platform for banks to upgrade their tech infrastructure to serve SMBs better
- Based in London but strategically focused on US market for greatest impact potential
- Launched initially with Metro Bank (UK), then expanded to Australia, Canada, and now primarily US
- Target customers are tier 1 banks (top 10 US banks) looking to improve merchant capabilities
  - Banks feel they’re being pushed out by new fintechs offering more digitized, accessible merchant services
  - Vision: large banks still maintain merchant trust and have massive merchant books through business banking relationships
  - Pollinate acts as nimble arm to help banks offer fintech-competitive solutions
- Joseph Cox works on product team but notes they’re “somewhat removed from direct customers” since they sell via banks as clients
  - Challenge: they hear what banks want rather than what merchants actually want
  - Would prefer speaking directly to merchants to understand their needs

### Pain points and needs

- Marketplace solution sounds like a better fit than embedded solution
  - Current platform already displays transactions and settlement views
  - More interested in extending capabilities to “order book against bank statements” - sees this as complementary fit
- Bank compliance and risk requirements slow down launches more than desired
- Product team wants to understand if SMB reconciliation pain is real or assumption
  - Quote: “I definitely want to be exploring if the target market of the SMB space is actually crying out for this stuff, or is this larger businesses that have more time to devote to this sort of reconciliation piece”
- Another product owner is leading reconciliation marketplace initiatives - Joseph is “probably open to a lot of options” but doesn’t know if reconciliation specifically highlighted yet

### Questions or concerns

- Potential overlap between existing Pollinate reconciliation functionality and proposed solution
  - Quote: “Is there a benefit? Is there a significant step up here that differentiates from what we offer today?”
- Questioning whether offering advanced reconciliation in marketplace makes sense if they already have reconciliation feature in portal
- Need to confirm merchant demand for more powerful reconciliation solution
- Integration capabilities - they only pull data from processors, not bank-side information
  - Interested if Rexi pulls data from other sources that could provide “enhanced proposition”

### Budget and timeline

- No specific budget or timeline information discussed
- Banks they target serve “tens, if not hundreds of thousands of merchants”
- Revenue model: reseller agreements or banks upselling their own products for direct kickbacks
- Marketplace aims to provide mechanism for banks to increase top line through merchant upsells
