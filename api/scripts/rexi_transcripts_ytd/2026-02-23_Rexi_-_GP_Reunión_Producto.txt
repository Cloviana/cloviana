### Global Processing Adquirencia Overview

- Antonio Tunno - Product Owner de Adquirencia
- Works with agrupadores (aggregators), not direct merchants
- 20 agrupadores managing approximately 250,000 sub-comercios
- GP liquidates to agrupador, agrupador liquidates to each sub-comercio
- Agrupadores work with their own proprietary dashboards

### Daily Process Flow

- End of day: GP sends presentations to card brands
  - IPM to Mastercard
  - CTF to Visa
- During interim: Generate files for agrupadores
  1. 8501 (2, 3, and 4) - payment summary files
  2. P300 - presented transactions
  3. 8510 - electronic liquidation
  4. COELSA file for payment processing
- Morning: Receive IPM/CTF responses from brands
- Agrupador receives payment and distributes to sub-comercios
- Only 2 direct merchant clients currently

### Reconciliation Pain Points & Opportunities

- “Si salen cosas, son muy esporádicas” - issues are sporadic
- No significant processing rejections currently
- Agrupador faces biggest challenge with tax complexity across different jurisdictions
- Emisión side works in reverse: receives IPM/CTF and sends funds to acquirers
- Potential value-add service for GP clients (both agrupadores and issuers)
- Need direct client conversations to understand specific reconciliation pain points
