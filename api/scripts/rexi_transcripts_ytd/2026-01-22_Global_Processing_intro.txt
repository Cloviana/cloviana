### Global Processing Overview

- Nicolas Mobilia - CPO at Global Processing
- Mariela Ferrer - Strategic Partnerships
- Payment processor handling both issuing and acquiring
  - Clients that issue cards
  - Acquirers processing merchant transactions
  - Direct merchants
- Currently in negotiations with Symmetric for reconciliation solution
  - Symmetric pricing model doesn’t scale for GP’s transaction volume
  - 50-60M transactions monthly in acquiring alone
  - Similar volume in issuing

### Business Model & Partnership Approach

- Preferred approach: white label solution but open to reseller model
- Two-pronged strategy:
  1. Internal use first - “want to be users of the tool before selling it”
  2. Then offer to clients as value-added service
- Current pain points in acquiring reconciliation processes
- Issuing market more challenging
  - Consolidated clients difficult to change
  - Would need aggressive pricing or significant functionality advantage

### Technical Architecture & Data Access

- Generate extensive outbound files for clients
- Clients currently interpret files and reconcile with own data
- Vision: GP handles reconciliation work for clients since they know the required processes
- Data warehouse integration preferred
  - Direct connection to avoid file uploads
  - Access to full 300+ fields vs. current 100 in standard files
  - Uses Snowflake for business intelligence
- Infrastructure requirement: deployment in GP’s cloud to manage costs at their transaction volumes

### Reconciliation Use Cases

- Acquiring side reconciliation:
  - Receive payments from card brands with summary reports
  - Match against detailed transaction files generated 10+ days prior
  - Verify accuracy of brand payments received
- Merchant payment reconciliation:
  - Different payment schedules for various transaction types
  - Daily validation: “Should I pay what I’m paying?”
  - Identify discrepancies and root causes
- Enhanced client value through direct database access vs. file-based processing
