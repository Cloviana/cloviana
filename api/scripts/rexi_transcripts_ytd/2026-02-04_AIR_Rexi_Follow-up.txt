SUMMARY
============================================================
### Rexi’s Business Models

- Two deployment options for reconciliation platform:
  - Pure SaaS: Software-based, charge per records reconciled
  - On-premise: One-time upfront fee + low retainer for support
    - Banks maintain full data control
    - Same connectivity to ecosystem partners
    - Eliminates vendor dependency concerns

### Crypto & Blockchain Applications

- Serving custodial crypto exchanges (95% of crypto usage)
  - Latin American crypto exchange (current customer)
  - US crypto exchange (pilot testing)
- Reconciliation approach:
  1. Bank ledger vs crypto exchange records
  2. Exchange ledger vs blockchain transactions
- True decentralized wallets don’t need reconciliation (blockchain is auditable)
- L2/L3 developments create new reconciliation needs beyond base blockchain layer

### Core Banking System Challenges

- Major pain point but creates opportunity with AI advancement
- Data quality issues and gatekeeper resistance
  - Some cores block external vendor access
  - Prefer building internal solutions (underestimate complexity)
- Progressive banks upgrading infrastructure:
  - Celtic, Thread, Cross River modernizing tech stacks
  - New tech-first sponsor banks emerging (Column example)
- Gap between old cores and AI capabilities driving innovation

### Partnership Opportunities with AIR

- Three collaboration areas identified:
  1. Co-author white papers for regulatory positioning
  2. Participate in AIR events for distribution to decision makers
  3. Partner in regulator meetings (OCC, FDIC, CFPB)
- AIR’s model: Non-member nonprofit seeking funders for convening activities
- Potential inclusion in annual RegTech Capitol Hill event
  - Tech companies meet regulators and funders
  - Networking focused, not endorsement

### Next Steps

- AIR team will discuss internally and identify right partnership fit
- Ignacio to follow up after meeting to advance collaboration
- AIR to add Rexi to RegTech Capitol Hill event consideration list
- Flexible approach: AIR to drive priorities, Rexi to adapt and support
