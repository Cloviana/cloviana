# Cloviana Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Cloviana Ltd (“Cloviana”, “the App”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares personal information when you install or use Cloviana in connection with your Shopify store.

Cloviana provides an AI-powered SEO content service that turns your Shopify products and catalog into search-optimized blog posts and related content to help you attract organic traffic and increase sales (“the Service”).

***

## Personal information we collect

### Information accessed via Shopify

When you install Cloviana, the App is granted access to certain data in your Shopify account through the following Shopify API scopes:

- `read_products`, `write_products`: Access and update product information so content can reference accurate product details and links.
- `read_content`, `write_content`: Access and publish blog posts, pages, and other content to your online store.
- `read_themes`, `write_themes`: Read and update theme-related content or templates as needed to support how generated content appears on your storefront.
- `read_online_store_navigation`, `write_online_store_navigation`: Read and update navigation menus so generated content can be correctly linked or organized.
- `read_locales`: Access localization settings to generate or display content in appropriate languages or locales.
- `read_metaobjects`, `write_metaobjects`: Access and manage metaobjects to store or structure generated content and related SEO data.
- `read_script_tags`, `write_script_tags`: Manage script tags needed to deliver parts of the Service, such as analytics or app integrations.

Through these scopes, we may access the following types of data from your Shopify store:

- Store catalog information, including products, collections, and related metadata.
- Existing blog posts, pages, and articles.
- Storefront navigation structure and relevant theme or content settings.

At this time, Cloviana does not intentionally use Shopify customer or order scopes, and the Service is designed to operate on catalog and content data rather than individual buyers’ personal information.

### Information collected directly from you

In addition to data accessed through Shopify APIs, Cloviana collects the following information directly from you when you connect your store or use the Service (for example, via app.cloviana.com):

- Store name and Shopify store URL.
- Store-associated contact email address (for example, the owner/admin email you connect to the app).
- Any preferences or configuration settings you provide inside the app (such as content settings, SEO preferences, or scheduling options).

### Usage data and analytics

When you use the App, we also collect certain technical and usage information to help operate and improve the Service, which may include:

- Device and browser information (such as browser type and version).
- IP address and general location inferred from IP.
- Dates and times of access, app pages visited, and actions taken in the app interface.
- Aggregated information about how generated content is used or performs (for example, which features are used most frequently).

We use analytics tools to collect and analyze some of this usage data for our own analytics.

### Cookies and similar technologies

Cloviana may use cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage or tags) in the app interface to:

- Keep you signed in and manage your session.
- Remember your preferences and settings.
- Understand how the App is used, so we can improve performance and usability.

You can usually configure your browser to refuse all or some cookies, or to alert you when cookies are being set. If you disable or refuse cookies, some parts of the App may not function properly.

## How we use your personal information

Cloviana uses the information described above for the following purposes:

- To provide and operate the Service, including:
    - Generating AI-powered SEO content (such as blog posts) based on your catalog and store configuration.
    - Publishing or updating content in your Shopify store in accordance with your instructions.
    - Configuring navigation or metaobjects where needed to deliver the Service.
- To communicate with you, including:
    - Sending service-related messages, onboarding information, and updates about new features or changes to the App.
    - Responding to your support requests, questions, or feedback.
- To maintain and improve the App, including:
    - Monitoring performance, usage, and trends.
    - Debugging, troubleshooting, and enhancing reliability and security.
- To manage our business, including:
    - Administration, billing, and account management on our own platform.
    - Enforcing our terms and policies and protecting our rights.

Cloviana does not use individual store customers’ personal information for its own marketing purposes, and the Service is currently designed to work primarily with store catalog and content data.

## AI processing and third-party service providers

To deliver the AI functionality and supporting infrastructure for Cloviana, we rely on third-party providers that process data on our behalf, under appropriate contractual safeguards:

- **AI providers:**
    - Groq: Used to run large language model inference for generating content quickly.  
    - Gemini (Google): Used to support certain AI functionality.

- **Hosting and infrastructure:**
    - Microsoft Azure: Used to host and operate the Cloviana application and related infrastructure in data centers located in the United States.

- **Email and communications:**
    - SMTP2GO: Used to send transactional emails related to your use of the Service.
    - Smartlead: Used for email outreach to merchants, for example to share information about Cloviana or invite prospective users to try the Service.

- **Analytics:**
    - Analytics services may be used to collect and analyze usage information about how users interact with the App.

Cloviana does not intentionally send store customer personal information (such as buyers’ names, email addresses, or shipping addresses) to AI providers as part of content generation. The prompts and data sent to AI providers are primarily based on catalog, content, and configuration data you maintain in your store or set in the App.

## Data retention and deletion

Cloviana retains personal information for as long as necessary to provide the Service to you and to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. In general:

- Merchant account information (such as store name and contact email), configuration data, and content generated through the App are retained while your account remains active.
- When you delete your Cloviana account or uninstall the App and instruct us to remove your data, Cloviana deletes or anonymizes associated merchant account information and generated content stored on our systems, subject to any data we must retain for legal, accounting, or security purposes.
- Technical logs and analytics data may be retained for a limited period to support security, troubleshooting, and analytics and are then deleted or anonymized.

If you would like to request deletion of personal information associated with your use of Cloviana, you can contact us at support@cloviana.com.
